Tier 1 knowledge: Stealth and general utility

/datum/heretic_knowledge/codex_cicatrixThis allows heretics to choose if they want to rush all the influences and take them stealthily, or Construct a codex and take what's left with more points. Another downside to having the book is strip searches, which means that it's not just a free nab, at least until you get exposed - and when you do, you'll probably need the faster drawing speed. Overall, it's a tradeoff between speed and stealth or power.
